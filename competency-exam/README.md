# Logic Competency Exam Preparation

This page is for students using the repository to prepare for the University at Buffalo Applied Ontology **Logic Competency Exam**.

Students enrolled in PHI 215 for Fall 2026 should instead begin with the [Fall 2026 course page](../course/README.md).

## Purpose

The Logic Competency Exam assesses material equivalent to that covered in an undergraduate symbolic logic course through first-order predicate logic with identity.

Relevant skills include:

- symbolization;
- propositional logic;
- truth tables;
- validity;
- natural deduction;
- direct, conditional, and indirect derivations;
- quantificational logic;
- proofs of invalidity;
- semantic interpretations;
- identity; and
- formal properties and selected extensions of predicate logic.

The existing [`Exams`](../Exams/) directory contains practice exam material and solutions.

## Exam Logistics

The exam will contain material equivalent to that found in the UB PHI 215 undergraduate symbolic logic course, i.e. up to first-order predicate logic with identity, symbolizations, derivations, proofs of invalidity, semantic interpretations, etc.

You will be contacted by a faculty member who will schedule a time to proctor the exam for students. 
 
You will have one hour to complete the exam; it will be taken in person. In extraordinary circumstances, you may be permitted to take the exam at the regularly scheduled time over zoom.

## Readings

Use the shared [Readings and Video Lectures](../resources/README.md).

The Hardegree material is especially useful for symbolization and proof theory. Bennett is especially useful for semantic interpretations and first-order logic.

## How to Prepare

1. Work through the material in order.
2. Do problems rather than merely reading examples.
3. Check that you can translate in both directions between English and logical notation.
4. Practice constructing derivations without automated proof checkers.
5. Practice semantic interpretations and proofs of invalidity.
6. Use the practice exams only after you have worked through the underlying material.
7. Review every mistake before attempting another practice exam.
