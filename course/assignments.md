# PHI 215 Symbolic Logic — Assignments and Exams

All graded assignments are submitted through **Microsoft Forms**.

## Submission Policy

All homework assignments and exams listed below are **open throughout the semester**.

Students may submit an assignment whenever they are prepared to do so. Students are nevertheless strongly encouraged to complete the assignments in numerical order because symbolic logic is cumulative and later material assumes mastery of earlier material.

Do not submit assignments by email unless specifically instructed to do so.

Students using this repository solely to prepare for the Logic Competency Exam do **not** need to submit these Forms unless specifically instructed to do so.
