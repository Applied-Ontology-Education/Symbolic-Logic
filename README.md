# Symbolic Logic

This repository supports two related uses:

1. **Logic Competency Exam preparation** — a self-paced pathway for students preparing to demonstrate competency in symbolic logic.
2. **PHI 215 Symbolic Logic, Fall 2026** — the asynchronous, remote, credit-bearing University at Buffalo course.

The underlying readings, lectures, and logic topics are shared, but the two pathways are different.

## Choose Your Path

### Preparing for the Logic Competency Exam

Start here:

**[Logic Competency Exam Preparation](competency-exam/README.md)**

Use this pathway if you are studying independently for the University at Buffalo Applied Ontology Logic Competency Exam..

### Taking PHI 215 for Credit

Start here:

**[PHI 215 Symbolic Logic](course/README.md)**

The Fall 2026 course is **asynchronous and remote**. It is organized into 15 weekly modules.

Students taking the course for credit should also use:

- **[Fall 2026 Schedule](course/schedule.md)**
- **[Assignments and Exams](course/assignments.md)**

All homework assignments and exams are submitted through Microsoft Forms. The Forms are open throughout the semester, so students may submit whenever they are prepared to do so. The weekly schedule identifies the recommended point in the course sequence for completing each assessment.
